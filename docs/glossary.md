# Glossary

Terms you'll encounter in Drips Wave programs.

## A

**Application**
A contributor's request to work on an issue. Submitted via the Drips Wave app or a GitHub comment. Does not guarantee assignment.

**Assignment**
Official approval from a maintainer to work on an issue. **Do not start work until assigned.**

## B

**Base Points**
The fixed 100 points every issue starts with, before complexity bonuses are added.

## C

**Code Metrics Scorecard**
A summary of your public GitHub activity (merged PRs, merge rate, OSS activity score) shown to maintainers when you apply.

**Compliment**
A bonus awarded by maintainers for exceptional work. Adds points and signals quality.

**Complexity**
The difficulty level assigned to an issue: Trivial, Medium, or High. Determines point value.

**Contributor**
A developer who applies for, claims, and resolves issues in a Wave.

## D

**Drips Wave**
A recurring bounty cycle where contributors resolve scoped issues and earn USDC rewards based on points.

**Drips Wave App**
The web application at drips.network for browsing Waves, applying for issues, and managing rewards.

## E

**Ecosystem**
The broader open-source community a Wave Program serves (e.g., Stellar, Filecoin).

## F

**Featured Repository**
A repository with a points multiplier applied. Check the issue for multiplier details.

## G

**Good First Issue**
A label indicating an issue is suitable for newcomers — clear scope, limited dependencies, helpful context.

## H

**High Complexity**
Issues worth 200 points. Complex features, refactors, comprehensive guides.

## K

**KYC (Know Your Customer)**
Identity verification required before applying for issues. Regulatory requirement for reward distribution.

## L

**Leaderboard**
Real-time ranking of contributors by points earned in a Wave. Higher rank = larger payout share.

## M

**Maintainer**
The person responsible for a repository. Reviews applications, assigns contributors, reviews PRs, and can award Compliments.

**Medium Complexity**
Issues worth 150 points. Standard feature work, new sections, examples.

**Multiplier**
A factor applied to points for featured repositories. Multiplies base + complexity bonus.

## P

**Pending Application**
An application that hasn't been accepted or rejected. Counts toward your 15-application limit.

**Points**
Units representing contribution value. Convert to rewards based on your share of total Wave points.

**PR (Pull Request)**
A request to merge your changes into a repository. The vehicle for your contribution.

**Program Organizer**
The entity running a Wave Program (e.g., Stellar Development Foundation). Sets the reward budget and approves repositories.

## R

**Reward Budget**
The total USDC pool for a Wave. Distributed proportionally based on points earned.

**Reward Grant**
Your earned portion of the reward pool, withdrawable to a Stellar wallet.

## S

**Scope**
The specific work defined in an issue. Stay within scope — don't add unrelated changes.

**Stellar**
The blockchain network where Drips Wave rewards are paid (USDC on Stellar).

**Stellar Wave**
The Wave Program run by the Stellar Development Foundation.

## T

**Trivial Complexity**
Issues worth 100 points. Typos, broken links, minor copy changes.

**Trustline**
A Stellar wallet setting that allows it to hold USDC. **Required to receive rewards.**

## U

**USDC**
USD Coin, the stablecoin used for Drips Wave rewards. Paid on the Stellar network.

## W

**Wave**
A specific sprint within a Wave Program. Typically one week per month.

**Wave Label**
A GitHub label (e.g., `Stellar Wave`) that marks an issue as part of a Wave.

**Wave Program**
A long-running initiative around an ecosystem that spawns recurring Waves.

## X

**XLM**
Stellar's native cryptocurrency. You need a small amount to activate a Stellar wallet.

## Withdrawal-Related Terms

**Test Transaction**
A $1 withdrawal to verify your wallet is configured correctly before requesting the full grant.

**Expiration**
Reward grants have expiration dates. Check the grant details and withdraw before expiry.

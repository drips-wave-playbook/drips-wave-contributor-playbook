# Drips Wave Contributor Playbook

A practical, open-source handbook for contributors joining Drips Wave programs. Learn how to find issues, claim them, earn points, and get recognized — all while building a verifiable portfolio of open-source work.

## Who This Is For

- **New contributors** who want to earn their first Drips Wave points
- **Experienced developers** looking to maximize rewards and contribution quality
- **Maintainers** who need clear contributor guidance to share with applicants
- **Ecosystem organizers** who want to understand how contributor onboarding works

## How to Use This Playbook

1. **Read the getting-started guide** to set up your Drips Wave account and Stellar wallet.
2. **Browse the docs** relevant to your immediate challenge (finding issues, writing applications, etc.).
3. **Use the checklists** in `templates/` before submitting applications or PRs.
4. **Contribute back** — if you find gaps or improvements, open an issue or PR.

## Contributing

We welcome contributions of all sizes. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for how to claim issues, the Drips Wave points system, and our PR process.

All contributors are expected to follow our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
